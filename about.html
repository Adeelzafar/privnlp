{{ site.title }} is a five-year research line on privacy risks and defences for language models at the School of Information Technology, Halmstad University, Sweden. We study how models memorize and leak personal information, how to test for it, and how to build models, datasets and detectors that meet the requirements of the GDPR and the EU AI Act. Our main application area is clinical text.
