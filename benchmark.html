Metrics and results come from _data/benchmark.yml. {%- endcomment -%} {%- capture method -%} ## How it works We plant fictional patients into a clinical fine-tuning corpus. Each planted patient has identifiers (name, record number, date of birth) and an associated condition. Half of them go into the training set as **members**; the other half are held out as **non-members** and never seen by the model. After fine-tuning, we attack the model in two ways: 1. **Asking**: targeted prompts that name a patient and ask about them, the way a curious user or insider might. 2. **Sampling**: drawing many unprompted or lightly prompted generations and searching them for planted patients. Comparing members with non-members separates genuine memorization from lucky guesses. All patients are synthetic, so the benchmark can be shared openly without exposing anyone. ## What we measure {%- endcapture -%}
